CrimeSearch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 August 2026

CrimeSearch may contain confidential investigation information and personal data. Use it only for authorised work and follow your organisation’s policies and applicable law.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CrimeSearch handles information when you use the CrimeSearch application. CrimeSearch is an investigation and case-management tool intended for authorised users. The organisation operating your CrimeSearch account is responsible for deciding which case information may be entered and who may access it.

2. Information we process

Depending on how your organisation uses CrimeSearch, the app may process account details such as your email address, user ID and access role; incident and investigation records; suspect and vehicle details; notes, evidence descriptions, timelines and links between records; and photographs or other images uploaded by authorised users. Investigation records may contain highly sensitive personal information supplied by your organisation or its users. Do not upload information unless you are authorised to do so.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, enforce administrator/member permissions, create and manage investigation records, associate suspects and vehicles with incidents, display uploaded images, support search and investigation workflows, maintain application security and reliability, and provide the service to the organisation that manages your account.

4. Service providers and storage

CrimeSearch uses Firebase Authentication to sign users in and Firebase Cloud Firestore to store application records and account-role information. Images may be uploaded to Cloudinary and their image URLs stored with the relevant records. These providers process information on behalf of the service and under their own terms and privacy documentation. Data may be processed on servers located in countries other than your own. Your organisation should ensure its use of CrimeSearch complies with applicable law and its own data-handling obligations.

5. Sharing and access

CrimeSearch does not sell personal information. Records are available to authenticated users according to the application permissions and the organisation’s configured access rules. Administrators may have broader access than members. Information may also be processed by the service providers described above, or disclosed where required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to investigate misuse or security incidents. Anyone who receives an image URL may potentially be able to access that image if the hosting configuration makes it publicly accessible; do not treat an unsigned/public image URL as a confidential access control.

6. Retention and deletion

Records are retained for as long as the organisation operating CrimeSearch requires them for legitimate operational, legal or evidentiary purposes. Deleting a record from CrimeSearch may not automatically delete a separately stored image from Cloudinary or another provider. The organisation’s administrator should coordinate deletion of both the application record and any corresponding hosted files, subject to legal retention requirements.

7. Security

CrimeSearch uses account authentication and role-based application controls. No internet-connected system or storage provider can guarantee absolute security. Organisations should use strong, unique credentials, restrict account access to authorised personnel, configure backend security rules correctly, and avoid sharing account credentials or sensitive image links.

8. Your choices and requests

Contact the organisation that issued your CrimeSearch account to request access, correction or deletion of information, report a privacy concern, or ask about the organisation’s retention practices. Your organisation may be required to retain some records for legal, regulatory or evidentiary reasons. You can stop using the app and sign out at any time.

9. Children

CrimeSearch is designed for authorised professional use and is not intended for children to use as a consumer service. Do not create an account unless you are authorised by the organisation operating CrimeSearch.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest effective version should be published at the public privacy-policy URL supplied in the Google Play Console.
